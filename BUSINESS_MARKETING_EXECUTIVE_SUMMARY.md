## What is SprueCrafter?

**SprueCrafter** is a professional desktop and web-based application that converts 3D models into industry-quality sprues optimized for resin 3D printing. It targets the intersection of two rapidly growing markets: 3D printing ($13.8B, growing 23.3% CAGR) and scale modeling ($2.1B market).

**Unique Value Proposition:** The only specialized tool for automated sprue generation with AI-powered features, delivering professional results (rivaling Meng/Takom manufacturers) at an accessible price point.

---

## Business Model

### Revenue Model: Freemium SaaS

**Free Tier (Forever Free):**
- All core features including sprue generation
- Unlimited conversions
- All file formats
- Community support
- **Goal:** Rapid user acquisition and market penetration

**Pro Tier ($10/month or $100/year):**
- Priority support
- Advanced batch processing
- API access
- Early feature access
- Cloud storage (5GB)
- Commercial use license
- **Goal:** 5-10% conversion rate from free users

### Additional Revenue Streams (Future)
- Enterprise licensing ($500-2,000/month)
- API access tiers ($50-200/month)
- White-label licensing ($10K-50K per license)
- Educational licensing ($500-2,000 per institution)

## Marketing Strategy

### Overall Approach: Community-Led Growth with Freemium Conversion

**Strategy Pillars:**
1. **Awareness:** Content marketing, SEO, social media presence
2. **Acquisition:** Community engagement, influencer partnerships
3. **Activation:** Excellent onboarding and first experience
4. **Engagement:** Continuous value delivery and education
5. **Conversion:** Free-to-Pro nurture campaigns
6. **Retention:** Regular updates, excellent support
7. **Advocacy:** User success stories and community building

### Marketing Budget Year 1: $20,000

**Budget Allocation:**
- Influencer Partnerships: $5,000 (25%)
- Paid Advertising: $6,000 (30%)
- Content Creation: $3,000 (15%)
- Tools & Software: $2,000 (10%)
- Community & Events: $1,500 (7.5%)
- Other: $2,500 (12.5%)

### Primary Marketing Channels

1. **Content Marketing (30% of acquisition)**
   - Blog: 2 posts/week, SEO-optimized
   - YouTube: 1 video/week, tutorials and demos
   - Documentation: Comprehensive guides and resources

2. **Social Media (25% of acquisition)**
   - Reddit (Primary): r/resinprinting, r/modelmakers, r/3Dprinting
   - YouTube: Video content and community engagement
   - Instagram: Visual showcases and tips
   - Twitter/X: Updates and community interaction
   - Facebook: Group participation

3. **Community Engagement (15% of acquisition)**
   - Active participation in existing communities
   - Discord server (Launch Q2 2026)
   - Forum engagement
   - User-generated content support

4. **SEO (20% of acquisition)**
   - Target keywords: "sprue generation," "3d printing preparation"
   - On-page optimization
   - Link building through partnerships
   - Goal: First page ranking by Q3

5. **Influencer Marketing (20% of acquisition)**
   - Tier 1: 2-3 major creators (100K+ subs) - $2-3K each
   - Tier 2: 5-10 mid-level (20-100K) - $500-1K each
   - Tier 3: 15-20 micro (5-20K) - $100-300 each
   - Focus: YouTube 3D printing and modeling channels

6. **Paid Advertising (10% of acquisition, starting Q2)**
   - Google Ads: $300/month (search campaigns)
   - Facebook/Instagram: $200/month (interest targeting)
   - YouTube Ads: $100/month (video discovery)
   - Target: <$50 CAC for Pro subscribers

### Key Partnerships

**Strategic Partnerships:**
- 3D Printer Manufacturers (Elegoo, Anycubic, Phrozen)
- STL Marketplaces (MyMiniFactory, Cults3D, Thingiverse)
- Slicer Software (Chitubox, Lychee integration)
- Resin Manufacturers (co-marketing opportunities)

## Risk Management

### Key Risks & Mitigation

**Business Risks:**
- **Low conversion rates:** Strong value prop, regular features, excellent free tier
- **Competition:** Focus on niche, continuous innovation, superior sprue quality
- **Slow adoption:** Diversify marketing, gather feedback, pivot if needed

**Technical Risks:**
- **Performance issues:** Algorithm optimization, cloud processing for heavy tasks
- **Compatibility:** Extensive testing, fallback conversions, clear documentation
- **Security:** Regular audits, updates, secure coding practices

**Market Risks:**
- **Market contraction:** Diversify into related markets, international expansion
- **Technology disruption:** Stay current, invest in R&D, flexible architecture
